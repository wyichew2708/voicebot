# Tiq

The direct online channel of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. Brand is a channel
attribute, not a product identity: coverage, limits and exclusions are the
same product however it was bought. Only three things vary by channel -- the
purchase route, the applicable promotion, and the contact route.

## Contact route

Customer Care is reachable on WhatsApp
([source](../../raw/faq/tiq-travel-covid19-addon.md#p4)). The number is
recorded on this page rather than in each product page, so that a change is
one edit.

Note the narrow evidence: the citation is a travel FAQ. Treating it as the
contact route for every product is an assumption, which is why this page is
`medium` confidence and carries no spoken wording for other products.

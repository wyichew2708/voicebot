# Building cover

Section 1 of the policy.

## What the building is

The private dwelling or residential flat and all domestic outbuildings,
fixtures and fittings attached to the building, the building structure but not
the foundations, the swimming pool, walls, gates and fences, and all other
domestic improvements of a structural nature at the premises for which the
policyholder is legally responsible
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p2)).

The wording also records a construction warranty: all buildings are declared
to be built of brick, stone or concrete and roofed with concrete, slate,
metal, asbestos or a composite, unless specially mentioned
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p2)).

## What is covered

Physical loss or damage to the building caused by an
[insured peril](../../../concept/insured-perils.md), up to the sum insured in
the schedule, in aggregate for any one policy year. Where more than one policy
with building cover is held with this insurer, compensation is payable under
one policy only
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

Building using eco-friendly materials, given as recycled steel and bamboo, is
deemed covered
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

## Boundary with renovation

The structure is building; the fixtures and fittings inside that are not part
of the structure are [renovation](renovation.md). They carry separate sums
insured, and [contents](contents.md) excludes anything already counted in
either.

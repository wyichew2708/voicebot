# Emergency cash allowance

Section 4 of the policy. Paid when an
[insured peril](../../../concept/insured-perils.md) renders the home
uninhabitable, as certified by the insurer
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

"Uninhabitable" is defined as the home being assessed by the insurer or its
appointed surveyor as unfit to live in or to be tenanted
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p4)).

| Extent | Homeowner or landlord | Tenant |
|---|---|---|
| Fully uninhabitable | {{table:tiq-home-emergency-cash-allowance:uninhabitable_100:homeowner}} | {{table:tiq-home-emergency-cash-allowance:uninhabitable_100:tenant}} |
| At least half uninhabitable | {{table:tiq-home-emergency-cash-allowance:uninhabitable_50:homeowner}} | {{table:tiq-home-emergency-cash-allowance:uninhabitable_50:tenant}} |

Below half uninhabitable there is no payout
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

These are the only figures in the home product that are fixed by the wording
rather than by the customer's schedule, which is why they are the only ones
in a benefit table. Approved claims are paid to the policyholder's digital
wallet on the insurer's app
([source](../../../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

# Time limit for notifying a claim

All claims and relevant supporting documents must be given to the insurer as
soon as possible, and no later than thirty days after the event that gives
rise to the claim
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-travel-2024-02-05-v8.md#p37)).

This is a deadline a caller can miss while waiting for a callback, which is a
reason for the bot to be able to answer it directly rather than book a call
for a colleague three days later.

## Scope

Travel only. The claims clause cited here is in the travel wording.

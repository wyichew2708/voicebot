# Pre-existing condition

Defined in the wording for non pre-existing plans as a condition for which the
insured person received medical treatment, diagnosis, consultation or
prescribed drugs within the twelve months immediately before the effective
date of insurance, or for which a medical practitioner recommended advice or
treatment in that same period
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-travel-2024-02-05-v8.md#p7)).

The spoken wording deliberately ends by handing the specific case to a person.
Applying this definition to a caller's actual medical history is an
underwriting judgement, and the bot has neither the facts nor the standing to
make it.

## Scope

Travel only, for the same reason as [the free look period](free-look.md).

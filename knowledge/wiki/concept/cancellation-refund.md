# Cancelling the policy

The policy may be cancelled at any time at the policyholder's written request,
and the insurer refunds eighty per cent of the pro-rata premium for the
unexpired period
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p18)).

This is distinct from the [free look period](free-look.md), which is a full
refund and applies only in the first fourteen days of a new policy. A caller
on a renewal call asking to cancel is almost always asking about this clause,
not that one, because the free look does not apply to renewals.

# Insured perils

Sections 1, 2 and 3 all pay for loss or damage "caused by any of the Insured
Perils", so this list is the gate every coverage question passes through
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p8)).

The perils include fire, lightning, thunderbolt and subterranean fire;
domestic explosion; aircraft and other aerial devices and articles dropped
from them; impact by a road vehicle not belonging to or controlled by the
policyholder or the household; bursting or overflowing of domestic water
tanks, apparatus or pipes; and theft accompanied by actual violent and
forcible entry or an attempt to break in
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p3)).

## Conditions inside the list

Two of them carry qualifications that a summary would lose, and the spoken
wording therefore hands specifics to a person. Water damage excludes damage to
the pipe or apparatus itself, and is excluded while the home is left
unoccupied for more than sixty days. Theft requires violent and forcible entry
or an attempt at it, so a theft without signs of break-in is a different
question
([source](../../raw/wordings/tiq-home-2025-03-15-v10.md#p3)).

This page lists the perils it can cite from the extracted text. The wording
continues the list beyond the items above, so an answer asserting that
something is *not* a peril must be checked against the document rather than
against this page.

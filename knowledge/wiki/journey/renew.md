# Renewing a policy

The renewal steps the bot describes on a call are the client's own call
script, not a fact drawn from a policy document, so they live in the script
and the fact store rather than here. This page exists to hold the compiled
version once an ops procedure document is ingested into `raw/sop/`.

Until then it carries no spoken wording, and the engine answers procedure
questions from the script as it always has.
